Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

DepositScope analyses public blockchain data to help licensed gambling operators understand the value and risk of a player's crypto wallet. This page explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to have it deleted.

The short version. We collect the email address you register with, a small amount of technical data needed to keep your account secure, and the wallet addresses our customers ask us to analyse. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising trackers or cookies (we count page views with a cookieless counter, see section 3), and we never post or read anything in your Google account beyond your email address at the moment you sign in.

1. Who we are

DepositScope ("we", "us") operates the website depositscope.com and the product at app.depositscope.com. For privacy questions, write to hello@depositscope.com.

2. Two different roles

We handle two kinds of data, and the law treats them differently.

As controller — your account with us

When you register, sign in, or contact us, we decide what is stored and why. This section covers that data.

As processor — data about your players

When a gambling operator sends us a wallet address and its own internal player identifier, the operator remains responsible for that data. We process it only to produce the verdict the operator asked for, under the operator's instructions and our Terms. We do not use it to build profiles for anyone else, and we never share one customer's data with another.

4. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us two things: your email address and whether Google has verified it. That is the entire scope we request (openid and email).

  • We do not request, receive, or store access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service.
  • The short-lived token Google issues is used once, in that moment, to read your email address. We do not store it and we do not keep a refresh token.
  • We use your email address only to identify your account — the same way a password login would. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • If the email address is new to us on the client sign-in page, a free trial account is created for it. On the admin console, Google only recognises accounts we invited.

Google's own handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Steps at your request before a contract — contacting you about the demo you asked for.
  • Contract — creating your account, signing you in, producing verdicts you request.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse of the free trial, and knowing when someone registers so we can respond to them.
  • Legal obligation — keeping records required for accounting and, where applicable, sanctions screening.

7. Blockchain data cannot be deleted

Blockchain transfers are public records maintained by thousands of independent computers. We store a copy so verdicts can be recalculated without querying the networks again, but we cannot erase a record from the blockchain itself — nobody can. What we can and do delete is everything that links a wallet address to a person or to a customer's player account. Once that link is gone, the address is as anonymous to us as it is to any other observer.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Write to hello@depositscope.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your national data protection authority.

If you are a player, not a customer: we hold data about you only because a gambling operator asked us to analyse a wallet. That operator decides what happens to it, so please send your request to the operator. They can erase you from our systems at any time — the product has a dedicated deletion function that removes the player, their wallets, verdicts and alerts, and records that the erasure happened.

9. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with TLS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes.
  • Each customer's data is isolated by a tenant identifier enforced on every query.
  • Access to production is limited to our own team; database backups run nightly and are restore-tested monthly.

10. Children

The service is sold to licensed businesses only and is not directed at anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, email account holders.
